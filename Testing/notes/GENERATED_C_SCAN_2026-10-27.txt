What the counts mean in practice (read from the C):
  * Every Nat add/compare in the loops is `lean_nat_*` (inline scalar test + call on overflow) with a lean_dec on
    the old value; positions, counters and limits are Nat in every loop above.
  * a[i]! on `Array UInt64` → lean_array_get_borrowed (bounds test + panic default) + lean_unbox_uint64 (pointer
    chase to a boxed UInt64). ByteArray.get! → lean_byte_array_get (bounds test on a Nat index).
  * cnt64 ends in `% 255` (lean_uint64_mod): one 64-bit division by a constant per word (clang turns it into a
    multiply-high + shifts), and the SWAR form is not the idiom clang rewrites to popcnt.
  * No lean_nat_big_* calls inside loop bodies on the hot path; the "big" column is usize/uint64 → Nat conversions
    (lean_usize_to_nat etc.), mostly at loop exits.
