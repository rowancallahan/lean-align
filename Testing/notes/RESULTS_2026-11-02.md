## 3. Validation
- **Unique genome, exact match:** 4.0 Mb genome with a proved 15-mer checker (`windows_far_edits`, `windows_far_hamming`, `checkUnique_sound`; standard axioms; checker PASS on 7,999,860 15-mers); 670,178 pairs: all 1,340,356 mates identical to minibwa in chrom, POS, strand, CIGAR, FLAG, MAPQ, TLEN, SEQ, QUAL (`samtools view` columns 1-11 byte-identical), all `XU:Z:unique`. Easy regime by design: tests plumbing, not repeats [UNIQ Result, §2, §4].
- **Fuzz audits A-E = 0:** 3 seeds x mason/low/high x 10k pairs, hg38: AS recomputed from the reference (0 mismatches), XB >= AS1+AS2 (0), XB >= any minibwa proper pair's score under our scoring (0 of 89,992 scorable pairs), no `unique` with a proper minibwa pair elsewhere scoring >= ours (0), no `unique` with minibwa MAPQ 60 elsewhere and the truth agreeing with minibwa (0); mutated copies make the checks fire [FUZZ "Result in one line", "Audit rules"].
- **Real-data rescoring, 0 bugs:** NovaSeq/HiSeq 200k, the 2 + 5 pairs where both tools say unique at disjoint places: minibwa's placement scored in our model is >= ours in **0 of 7**; 449 / 170 mates where minibwa is strictly better at the same place are all labelled `unknown` by us, none unique [REAL §6, §2].
- **SAM/BAM:** zero violations in ~1.6 M records of the independent output checker except the placeholder alignments; BAM passes samtools quickcheck/flagstat/round trip [OUT_FUZZ Summary 2-3].
- **Trimming caveat:** in 4 of those 7 pairs we call unique on a trimmed read whose discarded low-quality 3' bases favour minibwa's copy on the full read; the claim is correct for the trimmed read, as proved, but depends on the trimming choice [REAL §6]. A read of all low quality is cut to one letter and still aligned (999 mates under 20 letters in the 3,041-pair edge set) [OUT_FUZZ §5]; the spec does not constrain the trimmer [SPEC finding 9].

## 5. Open
- **Report floor and "gave up" (Rowan's decision):** every reported pair gets a score floor of about -50; `junkScore` stays -16. Reads whose search stops early and whose best pair is below the floor have no valid answer; a "gave up" answer in the Spec is the open choice [SS reportFloor decision]. Degenerate last-resort pairs are 0.16-2.72% of pairs on the trimming head (0.16 / 0.99 / 1.19 / 2.72 % for low / mason / HiSeq / NovaSeq) [OUT_FUZZ §1]; 0.16-0.97% at 8b99433 [SS HEADLINE O1].
- **Index-load VM effect:** the shim reads a 5.3 GB index into memory; with minibwa's 7 GB index and 15 GB of RAM the page cache is reclaimed, and the shim's load varies 1.6-17 s per run, so wall-clock differences below ~10 s are noise; CPU is the steadier column [THR "Final proven head 1ec26a7"; N32 §4].
- **mmap of the index:** needs a `Main.lean` change (Rowan's file); the index is held twice in memory today and a cold load takes 5-30 s [SS Decisions open 5 and "Later (Rowan)"; THR "Next"]. The same limit blocks the extra short-seed index (5.8 GB) [SS Short-seed second pass].
