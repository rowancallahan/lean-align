## How `mapStep` runs (Proofs/MapFast.lean)
- **State:**
  - the job function;
  - per file, a carry with its newline scan;
  - per file, a queue of complete slices;
  - the pending `Task`s, in order.
- **`step (some (c1, c2))`:**
  - append each chunk to its carry and cut complete slices of 4·16000 lines (`advance`/`findNewline`, no parsing);
  - pair the queues in order and `Task.spawn (job a b)` for each pair;
  - while more than 8 jobs are pending, take the oldest result, in order (none → the step is none).
- **`step none`:** the unpaired slices plus the carries become one last job; every result is taken.
- **`job`:**
  - `parseFastqB` on both slices (= `FQ.parse`);
  - `prepPairsB`: equal counts, `qnameB` equal and `qnameOkB`, then `TrimCore.trimB`;
  - `alignPairsP` with `chunkSetup` built inside the job;
  - `samChunkS`.
- **Setup per job, not shared:** the aligner's setup is built inside each job. Shared from the main thread, all its reference counts became atomic, which cost +6% CPU at 1M. Building it once per 16000 pairs costs under 1%.
